Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I track my order?
You'll receive a WhatsApp message with your tracking link once your order is dispatched — usually within 1–2 business days. You can also use the tracker above.
Can I change my delivery address?
Yes — contact us within 12 hours of placing your order on WhatsApp or email and we'll update it before dispatch.
How do I return a product?
WhatsApp us with your order ID and reason. We'll arrange a reverse pickup within 48 hours. See our full return policy.
When will I get my refund?
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days once we receive the returned item. COD refunds go via UPI or bank transfer.
Do you offer bulk or gifting orders?
Yes! We offer custom pricing, branding, and packaging for bulk orders above 10 units. WhatsApp us or fill the form above with topic "Bulk / Gifting Order".
Is COD available everywhere in India?
Yes, COD is available pan-India with a ₹49 handling fee. A few remote pincodes may only support prepaid — you'll be notified at checkout if so.
