Shipping Policy

Last updated: 23 May 2025

🚚 Free standard shipping on all orders above ₹499. COD available pan-India.

Dispatch Timeline

All orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. Orders placed before 12:00 PM IST on business days are typically dispatched the same day.

Delivery Timeline

  • Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 2–4 business days
  • Tier 2 & Tier 3 cities: 4–6 business days
  • Remote / rural areas: 5–8 business days

Shipping Charges

  • Standard delivery — Free on orders above ₹499
  • Standard delivery — ₹49 on orders below ₹499
  • Express delivery (1–2 days) — ₹99
  • Cash on Delivery — ₹49 handling fee (regardless of order value)

Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a WhatsApp message with your tracking link and courier partner details. You can also track your order by contacting us directly on WhatsApp with your order ID.

Courier Partners

We ship through trusted courier partners including Delhivery, Shiprocket, and DTDC depending on your location. The courier partner may vary based on serviceability in your area.

Undelivered / Returned Shipments

If a shipment is returned to us due to an incorrect address, failed delivery attempts, or refusal at delivery, we will contact you via WhatsApp. Re-shipping charges of ₹49 will apply. For COD orders refused at delivery, a ₹99 return fee will be deducted from any future refund.

Have a shipping question? We're here to help.

Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 May 2025

✅ 7-day hassle-free returns. If you're not happy, we'll make it right.

Return Eligibility

You can return a product within 7 days of delivery if:

  • The item is unused and in its original packaging
  • The item is not damaged due to misuse
  • All tags, accessories, and packaging materials are intact

How to Initiate a Return

  • WhatsApp us at +91 73561 13346 with your order ID and reason for return
  • Or email us at hello@nooksy.in with photos of the item
  • We'll confirm your return request within 24 hours
  • Reverse pickup will be arranged within 48 hours of confirmation

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please share a photo/video within 48 hours of delivery. We will offer a full replacement or complete refund — at no extra cost to you, including reverse shipping.

Refund Timeline

  • Online payments (UPI / Cards / Net Banking): Refunded to original payment method within 5–7 business days
  • COD orders: Refunded via UPI or bank transfer within 5–7 business days
  • You will receive a WhatsApp confirmation once the refund is initiated

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items returned after 7 days of delivery
  • Items that are used, damaged due to misuse, or missing original packaging
  • Bulk orders above ₹10,000 (these are final sale)
  • Customised or personalised products

Want to return something? Reach out and we'll sort it out quickly.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 May 2025

Nooksy ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a customer.

What Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Delivery address and billing address
  • Payment reference (we do not store card or UPI details)
  • Order history and purchase preferences
  • Device information and browsing behaviour on our website (via cookies)

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order updates via WhatsApp and email
  • To handle returns, refunds, and customer support queries
  • To send promotional offers and new arrivals (you can opt out anytime)
  • To improve our website and product selection

Payment Data

All payment transactions are processed securely by PayU. We do not store, access, or process your card, UPI, or net banking credentials. PayU's privacy policy governs the handling of your payment information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share limited data with:

  • Courier partners (name, phone, address) for order delivery
  • PayU for payment processing
  • WhatsApp / Meta for order update communications

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to remember your cart, preferences, and browsing session. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect cart functionality.

Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of incorrect data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@nooksy.in. We will process your request within 7 business days.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data from unauthorised access or disclosure. Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all data transmission.

Questions about your data? Email us at hello@nooksy.in
